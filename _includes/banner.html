Hi, I'm Koustav

Postdoctoral researcher · Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics (Albert Einstein Institute), Potsdam

I study the gravitational-wave astrophysics of compact binary mergers: building modelled searches for binary black holes, modelling the ringdown of the merger remnant, carrying out parameterised tests of General Relativity, shaping the science case for next-generation detectors, and inferring the properties of individual sources and their population.

Off duty I binge web series and anime, wrestle with Italian and German, cook out of necessity, and occasionally try not to fall off a treadmill at the local biomechanics lab.
