<section id="research">
  <header class="major">
    <h2>Research</h2>
  </header>
  <p>I work on the gravitational-wave astrophysics of compact binary mergers, from finding the signals to what they tell us about gravity and the Universe.</p>
  <div class="posts">
    <article>
      <span class="image"><img src="{{ 'assets/images/research/search.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Fitting factor of simulated signals across the template-bank parameter space" /></span>
      <h3>Modelled searches</h3>
      <p>Matched-filter and machine-learning based searches for compact binary mergers.</p>
      <p class="papers"><strong>Papers:</strong> <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00193" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Optimised PyCBC IMBH search</a> · <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.01654" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Higher-harmonics IMBH search</a> · <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02545" target="_blank" rel="noopener">THAMES transfer learning</a> · <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08332" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CNN detection significance</a></p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span class="image"><img src="{{ 'assets/images/research/ringdown.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Toy inspiral-merger-ringdown waveform with the ringdown highlighted" /></span>
      <h3>Ringdown modelling</h3>
      <p>Modelling the post-merger signal of binary black holes: calibrating analytic ringdown templates for effective-one-body waveforms against numerical relativity, and black hole spectroscopy.</p>
      <p class="papers"><strong>Papers:</strong> <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.17315" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Inspiral-merger informed ringdown templates</a></p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span class="image"><img src="{{ 'assets/images/research/tgr.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Posterior on fractional deviations of the remnant mass and spin from General Relativity for GW250114" /></span>
      <h3>Tests of General Relativity</h3>
      <p>Parameterised tests of gravity in the strong-field regime and hierarchical tests of GR.</p>
      <p class="papers"><strong>Papers:</strong> <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07070" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GWTC-4.0 mixture-model tests</a> · <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.04593" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GW250114 multipronged analysis</a> · <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19593" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Theory-agnostic parametrised model</a></p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span class="image"><img src="{{ 'assets/images/research/xg.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Gravitational-wave strain from one event in 2015 to the continuous signal expected with next-generation detectors" /></span>
      <h3>Next-generation detectors</h3>
      <p>Science case and network design for Cosmic Explorer, the Einstein Telescope and LIGO-India: detection rates, sky localisation, and what a continuous background of mergers means for analysis. I am the lead maintainer of <a href="https://koustavchandra.github.io/gwforge/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">gwforge</a>, a package for building mock data.</p>
      <p class="papers"><strong>Papers:</strong> <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10349" target="_blank" rel="noopener">LIGO-India in the XG era</a> · <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.22302" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Foreground signals in XG inference</a> · <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03463" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Neutron-star radii with XG detectors</a> · <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21109" target="_blank" rel="noopener">gwforge</a></p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span class="image"><img src="{{ 'assets/images/research/source.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Sketch of a binary black hole reference frame with orbital angular momentum, line of sight, kick direction and the angles between them" /></span>
      <h3>Source characterisation &amp; inference</h3>
      <p>Measuring what the signals encode: source properties, remnant properties, individual events, and population-level cosmological and astrophysical inference built on fast likelihoods and careful statistics.</p>
      <p class="papers"><strong>Papers:</strong> <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.03465" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Black-hole recoil from higher-order modes</a> · <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.03841" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GW230529 origins and remnant</a> · <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15150" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Eccentricity in disguise? GW231123</a> · <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14898" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Systematics in dark-siren cosmology</a></p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
