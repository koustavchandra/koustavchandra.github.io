<section id="research">
  <header class="major">
    <h2>Research</h2>
  </header>
  <p>I work on the gravitational-wave astrophysics of compact binary mergers, from finding the signals to what they tell us about gravity and the Universe.</p>
  <div class="posts">
    <article>
      <span class="image"><img src="{{ 'assets/images/research/search.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Fitting factor of simulated signals across the template-bank parameter space" /></span>
      <h3>Modelled searches</h3>
      <p>Matched-filter searches for binary black hole mergers, with a focus on intermediate-mass black holes and the higher-order harmonics they emit. I designed the optimised PyCBC IMBH search and the first search to use higher harmonics in its templates.</p>
      <ul class="actions">
        <li><a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.106.123003" class="button small" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Paper</a></li>
      </ul>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span class="image"><img src="{{ 'assets/images/research/ringdown.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Toy inspiral-merger-ringdown waveform with the ringdown highlighted" /></span>
      <h3>Ringdown modelling</h3>
      <p>Modelling the post-merger signal of binary black holes: calibrating analytic ringdown templates for effective-one-body waveforms against numerical relativity, and attaching them cleanly across the merger.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span class="image"><img src="{{ 'assets/images/research/tgr.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Posterior on fractional deviations of the remnant mass and spin from General Relativity for GW250114" /></span>
      <h3>Tests of General Relativity</h3>
      <p>Parameterised tests of gravity in the strong-field regime: black-hole spectroscopy, deviations in the remnant mass and spin, and fractional mode deviations, applied to the loudest events such as GW250114.</p>
      <ul class="actions">
        <li><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.04593" class="button small" target="_blank" rel="noopener">arXiv</a></li>
      </ul>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span class="image"><img src="{{ 'assets/images/research/xg.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Gravitational-wave strain from one event in 2015 to the continuous signal expected with next-generation detectors" /></span>
      <h3>Next-generation detectors</h3>
      <p>Science case and network design for Cosmic Explorer, the Einstein Telescope and LIGO-India: detection rates, sky localisation, and what a continuous background of mergers means for analysis. I maintain <a href="https://koustavchandra.github.io/gwforge/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">gwforge</a> for building mock data.</p>
      <ul class="actions">
        <li><a href="https://doi.org/10.3847/2041-8213/add15f" class="button small" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Paper</a></li>
      </ul>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span class="image"><img src="{{ 'assets/images/research/source.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Sketch of a binary black hole reference frame with orbital angular momentum, line of sight, kick direction and the angles between them" /></span>
      <h3>Source characterisation &amp; inference</h3>
      <p>Measuring what the signals encode: source properties and remnant kicks from higher-order modes, multimessenger prospects of individual events, and population-level cosmological and astrophysical inference built on fast likelihoods and careful statistics.</p>
      <ul class="actions">
        <li><a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41550-025-02632-5" class="button small" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Paper</a></li>
      </ul>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
